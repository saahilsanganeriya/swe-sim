The saved artifacts establish three valid scenarios and working offline grading of the tracked example, but they do not establish all of the pipeline's stronger guarantees. The reviewed snapshot had two P1 issues: interactive trials changed identity after Temporal planned their unit, and repeated withholding selections could remove requirements without recording their full text. Other material gaps affect probe reuse, control quality, build identity, scaffold separation, the console's disclosure/confirmation gates, and the interpretation of recorded measurements. The reports should not be used to claim a measured interaction benefit or complete reproducibility of the current pipeline.

For a stronger reward boundary, trusted assertions must ultimately be isolated from submitted Python rather than relying solely on mutually consistent reports.
